# Fees

A prediction costs one payment, made when you place it. The amount you enter is everything you pay, fee included.

## The fee

The fee is **5% of your amount**, shown on the ticket before you place it. On a $100 prediction, $5 is the fee and $95 buys the payout.

## What you never pay

- No fee to collect a payout. It is sent to your wallet.
- No cut of your winnings.
- No fee on swaps. Paying with USDC or USDT uses a rate from the third party routing the swap. See [Pay and swap](../trade/pay-and-swap.mdx).

## Network fees

Placing a prediction with USDG is one transaction on Arbitrum, paid in ETH from your wallet. Settlement is sent and paid for by Isometric's keeper. You only pay a network fee after the end time if you need to claim a payout yourself.

## Can the fee change

The fee can change within limits fixed in the contract code. A change applies only to predictions placed after it.
