# Place your first prediction

This walkthrough takes you from an empty wallet to an open prediction.

<Tip>

To try it without real money, turn on **Demo** in the app header.

</Tip>

<Steps>

<Step title="Connect a wallet" icon="lucide:wallet">

Open [app.isometric.fi](https://app.isometric.fi), go to **Predict**, and choose **Connect**. No invite is needed.

</Step>

<Step title="Fund it on Arbitrum" icon="lucide:coins">

Isometric runs on Arbitrum One. You need two things in your wallet there:

- **USDG, USDC, or USDT** to pay for predictions. Every prediction pays out in USDG, so USDC and USDT are swapped to it as you place it.
- **A little ETH** for network fees.

Holding money on another network? Open your account menu and choose **Swap (cross-chain)** to bring it to Arbitrum. See [Pay and swap](../trade/pay-and-swap.mdx).

</Step>

<Step title="Write your prediction" icon="lucide:sliders-horizontal">

The ticket reads as a sentence: **I think BTC will be above $105K on Nov 27, 9am**. Press any part of it to change it.

- **Market.** Pick another market.
- **above / below.** Flip whether you think the price ends higher or lower.
- **Target price.** Further from today's price pays more and is less likely. You can also press the chart to set it.
- **End time.** Pick a day and hour, from 12 hours to 7 days away.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter your amount" icon="lucide:banknote">

Enter what to spend under **You put in**, counted in USDG. It is everything you pay, fee included. Press the token beside it to pay with USDC or USDT. **If you're right** shows the payout.

</Step>

<Step title="Place it" icon="lucide:check">

Press **Predict** and confirm in your wallet. The prediction is open as soon as the transaction lands. It shows under the chart with the time left and whether it is **On track**, or how far the price still needs to move.

</Step>

<Step title="Wait for the end time" icon="lucide:hourglass">

Nothing else is required from you. A prediction cannot be closed early. At the end time, one Chainlink price decides it, and a payout is sent to your wallet in USDG, normally a minute or two later.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Before you predict

- Read [Know the risks](./risks.mdx). It is short.
- A prediction runs to its end time. There is no early exit.
- Never use money you cannot afford to lose in full.
