# Writers

A writer is anyone who takes the other side of Isometric trades and predictions. Every one goes out for quotes, and any writer can answer.

## How a request is quoted

1. **A trader asks** for a prediction, or for a long or a short with a term, an amount and a leverage. The request goes to every writer at once.
2. **Writers answer** with a signed price. The best one fills.
3. **The position opens** in one transaction. The trader pays the premium and the writer deposits the rest of the **Max payout**. The contract holds both until settlement.
4. **At expiry** one Chainlink price settles it. The trader is paid their payout, and the rest goes back to the writer.

## What you are writing

Every trade is a European, cash-settled option spread on the market's Chainlink price, paid in USDG.

| Trader opens | You sell      | Payoff to the trader at price `P`       | Max payout                     |
| ------------ | ------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Long         | A call spread | `contracts × (min(P, cap) − strike)⁺`   | `contracts × (cap − strike)`   |
| Short        | A put spread  | `contracts × (strike − max(P, floor))⁺` | `contracts × (strike − floor)` |

- **You quote only the strike.** The request fixes the side, expiry, cap or floor, notional and premium. The size follows from your strike: `contracts = notional ÷ strike`.
- **The best strike wins.** The lowest strike for a long, the highest for a short. With the cap or floor fixed, that quote pays the trader more in every outcome.
- **It is fully collateralised.** At fill the contract escrows the trader's premium and your top-up to the spread's full value. Nothing else is ever owed.
- **It settles once.** At expiry the trader is paid the payoff and the rest of the escrow returns to you, in the same transaction.

## Writing predictions

You can write predictions too. Under the hood a prediction is the same option spread with a narrow band. The cap or floor is the trader's target, and the strike sits 0.1% short of it, so the spread pays in full at the target and nothing 0.1% short of it.

| Trader predicts | You sell      | Strike           | Cap or floor |
| --------------- | ------------- | ---------------- | ------------ |
| Up              | A call spread | `target × 0.999` | `target`     |
| Down            | A put spread  | `target × 1.001` | `target`     |

- **You quote only the size.** The request fixes the side, expiry, strike, cap or floor, and premium. You choose `contracts`. The max payout is `contracts × (cap − strike)` for Up or `contracts × (strike − floor)` for Down. You deposit that payout minus the trader's premium.
- **The largest payout wins.** The quote with the most contracts pays the trader the most.
- **Payouts are capped.** One prediction pays at most 1,000 USDG, and at most 10 times its amount.
