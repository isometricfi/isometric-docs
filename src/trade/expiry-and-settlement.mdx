# The end time

An Isometric position has a fixed end. Between open and that end nothing is required from you, and nothing can be done to you.

## Choosing it

Drag the line marked **Ends** on the chart. Its label shows the day and hour, such as **Ends Tomorrow 9 AM**. Terms run from 12 hours to 7 days, counted from when you request the quote and **rounded up to the next whole hour**.

## While the position is open

The position cannot be closed, resized, transferred, or liquidated. It shows under the chart in **Open** and in **Portfolio**, with what it would pay if it ended now and its max payout.

## At the end time

The contract uses the **last Chainlink price published at or before the end time**. If that reading is too old, zero, or negative, the contract checks the first reading after the end time instead. That reading must be positive.

Once the end time has passed, anyone can submit the end price. Isometric runs a keeper that does it, normally within a minute or two. Until then the position shows **Ending**. Waiting does not change which price counts.

The same transaction pays both sides. Your payout goes straight to your wallet, and the rest goes back to the writer.

## After the end

| Status   | What happened                                                   | What you do                                        |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Paid     | The price ended on your side and the payout reached your wallet | Nothing                                            |
| Lost     | The price ended at or past your worthless price                 | Nothing. The position paid nothing                 |
| Won      | It paid out, but the transfer to your wallet failed             | [Claim it](./payouts-and-claims.mdx)               |
| Refunded | No end price was published within 14 days of the end time       | Nothing. What you paid, less the fee, was returned |

## What can delay a payout

- **The keeper is behind.** Anyone can submit the end price, so a stalled keeper delays a payout rather than blocking it.
- **The price feed is late.** If no price arrives within 14 days of the end time, both sides are refunded.
- **The network is down.** Nothing can be recorded until Arbitrum is producing blocks again.

None of these change what you are owed. The money backing your payout stays in the contract until it reaches you.
