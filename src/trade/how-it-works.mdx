# How it works

Every Isometric position is an option spread.

- **A long is a call spread.** It pays as the price rises above your worthless price, up to a cap.
- **A short is a put spread.** It pays as the price falls below your worthless price, down to a floor.
- **You buy the spread, a writer sells it.** Writers compete to quote your trade, and the best quote fills.
- **It is fully collateralised.** When the trade opens, the writer locks the spread's full value, your **Max payout**, in the contract.
- **It settles once, at expiry.** One Chainlink price decides the payout, paid in USDG. There is no early exercise and no early close.

## The ticket in options terms

| On the ticket         | In options terms                                |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Worthless at or below | The strike                                      |
| Max payout            | The spread's full value, between strike and cap |
| Position size         | The notional                                    |
| Max loss              | The premium plus the trading fee                |
| Settles               | The expiry                                      |

Leverage is the notional divided by what you pay. It sets the size of the spread, not a margin requirement, so there is nothing to liquidate.
