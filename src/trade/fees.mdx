# Fees

A position costs one payment, made when you open it. The amount you enter is everything you pay, fee included.

## The trading fee

The fee is **$0.50 plus 0.04% of your position size**. The ticket shows it before you confirm.

The fee comes out of the amount you enter, and the rest buys the position. So **Max loss** is the amount you entered, fee included. The fee goes to the Isometric treasury. The rest goes to the writer who takes the other side.

## What you never pay

| On a perp                  | On Isometric                     |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Funding every hour         | Nothing                          |
| Closing fee                | Nothing. There is no close       |
| Liquidation penalty        | Nothing. There is no liquidation |
| Withdrawal fee on a payout | Nothing                          |
| A cut of your winnings     | Nothing                          |

## Network fees

Opening a position is one transaction on Arbitrum or Robinhood Chain, paid in ETH from your wallet. Approving USDG is a signature, so it costs nothing.

Settlement is sent by Isometric's keeper, which pays its own network fees, and it pays winners straight to their wallets. You only pay a network fee after settlement if you ever need to [claim](./payouts-and-claims.mdx) a payout yourself.

## Can the fee change

The fee can change within limits fixed in the contract code. A change applies only to positions opened after it, never to one that is already open.
