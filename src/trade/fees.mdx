# Fees

A position costs one payment, made when you open it. The amount you enter is everything you pay, fee included.

## The trading fee

The fee is **$0.50 plus 0.04% of your position size**, and never more than 10% of the amount you enter. The ticket shows it before you confirm.

## What you never pay

| On a perp                  | On Isometric                     |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Funding every hour         | Nothing                          |
| Closing fee                | Nothing. There is no close       |
| Liquidation penalty        | Nothing. There is no liquidation |
| Withdrawal fee on a payout | Nothing                          |
| A cut of your profit       | Nothing                          |

## Network fees

Opening a position with USDG is one transaction on Arbitrum, paid in ETH from your wallet. Any approval is either a free signature or sent together with the trade.

Settlement is sent by Isometric's keeper, which pays its own network fees, and payouts go straight to wallets. You only pay a network fee after settlement if you ever need to [claim](./payouts-and-claims.mdx) a payout yourself.

## Swap costs

Isometric charges no fee on swaps, including when you pay for a trade with USDC or USDT. The rate comes from the third party routing the swap. If your wallet cannot batch transactions, paying with USDC or USDT also costs the network fees of the approval and the swap. See [Pay and swap](./pay-and-swap.mdx).

## Can the fee change

The fee can change within limits fixed in the contract code. A change applies only to positions opened after it, never to one that is already open.
