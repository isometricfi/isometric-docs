# Reading the quote

Every quote shows the same six numbers. They do not change meaning between markets, terms, or leverage. Once you can read them, you can read any Isometric trade.

## The six numbers

| Number                    | What it tells you                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Settles**               | The date and hour the position ends. One price at that hour decides the result. It cannot be closed before then.                |
| **Position size**         | Your amount times leverage. The exposure the position gives you.                                                                |
| **Worthless at or below** | For a long: settle at or below this price and you receive nothing. A short shows **Worthless at or above**.                     |
| **Profit above**          | For a long: settle above this price and you get back more than you paid. A short shows **Profit below**.                        |
| **Max payout**            | The most the position can pay in USDG. It is fixed when the trade opens.                                                        |
| **Max loss**              | Your amount, paid now including the trading fee. If the position expires worthless you lose it. No margin call, no liquidation. |

Between the worthless price and the profit price the position pays something, but less than it cost. That band is where you get part of your money back.

## Max payout

Every quote comes with a **Max payout**, fixed when the trade opens. Past a certain price the payout stops growing, so the writer can lock the whole amount in the contract when your trade opens. Longer terms leave more room to run, so their maximum is larger.

## A worked example

Bitcoin is at **$100,000**. You go **Long**, enter **$100**, choose **3 days** and **25x**. The ticket might show:

| Number                | Value                        |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Settles               | 3 days from now, on the hour |
| Position size         | $2,500                       |
| Worthless at or below | $97,400                      |
| Profit above          | $101,296                     |
| Max payout            | $323.40                      |
| Max loss              | $100.00                      |

The exact figures depend on the live price and the quotes writers send, so yours will differ.

Settlement at different prices pays:

| Bitcoin at settlement | You receive | Result |
| --------------------- | ----------- | ------ |
| $97,400 or below      | $0          | −$100  |
| $100,000              | $67         | −$33   |
| $101,296              | $100        | $0     |
| $104,000              | $169        | +$69   |
| $107,000              | $246        | +$146  |
| $110,000 and above    | $323        | +$223  |

Between $97,400 and $101,296 you get some money back but not all of it. Past $110,000 the payout stops growing: Bitcoin at $150,000 pays the same $323.

## The same idea as a short

Bitcoin is at $100,000. You go **Short**, enter **$80**, choose **1 day** and **25x**. The ticket might show:

| Number                | Value    |
| --------------------- | -------- |
| Position size         | $2,000   |
| Worthless at or above | $103,500 |
| Profit below          | $99,360  |
| Max payout            | $164.25  |
| Max loss              | $80.00   |

| Bitcoin at settlement | You receive | Result |
| --------------------- | ----------- | ------ |
| $103,500 or above     | $0          | −$80   |
| $101,000              | $48         | −$32   |
| $99,360               | $80         | $0     |
| $98,000               | $106        | +$26   |
| $95,000 and below     | $164        | +$84   |

In both examples the worthless price sits on the safe side of today's price and the profit price sits just past it. Most positions start like this: they have some value at open, and you come out ahead once the price moves a little further your way.

## Two shortcuts

**Each 1% past the worthless price is worth leverage percent of your amount.** At 25x, every 1% is worth 25% of what you entered: in the long example, about $25 per 1%.

**Breakeven is the Profit above price**, or **Profit below** for a short. It sits one leverage-share past the worthless price: 0.1% at 1000x, 1% at 100x, 4% at 25x, 10% at 10x, 20% at 5x.

## The quote follows the market

The quote updates as the price moves. Each quote is firm for a short time, and the app refreshes it before you sign. Your trade opens on exactly the quote you confirmed, or not at all. A failed trade costs only the network fee.
