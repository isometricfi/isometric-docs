# Know the risks

Isometric removes liquidation and funding. It does not remove the possibility of losing money. Read this page once before you trade.

<Warning>
  Every position can lose 100% of what you paid. Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose in
  full.
</Warning>

## You can lose everything you paid

A position that settles on the wrong side of its worthless price pays nothing. The higher the leverage, the closer that price sits to today's price and the more often this happens. The amount you paid is gone, with no partial refund.

## You cannot get out early

There is no close button. A position you regret at hour one still runs to its settlement time, and a position that is winning on day three can still lose on day seven. Only the settlement price counts. Positions cannot be transferred to another wallet either.

## One price at one hour decides

Settlement uses the last Chainlink price published at or before the settlement hour. A spike or dip that happens a minute later does not count. If you need a position that tracks the price continuously, this is not that product.

If the price feed had gone quiet for longer than its normal maximum gap before the hour, the first price published after the hour is used instead. That is a different price from the one you might expect, and it arrives later.

## Your payout is capped

Every position has a **Max payout**, fixed when it opens. However far the market runs, you receive that and no more. Shorter terms have a smaller maximum.

## The quote can move before you confirm

The quote follows the live price. Each quote is firm for a short time, and the app refreshes it before you sign. Your trade opens on exactly the quote you confirmed, or fails. A failed trade costs only the network fee.

## You may not get a quote

A trade needs a writer willing to take the other side. If no writer quotes your trade, or the market is paused, you cannot open a position at that moment. Existing positions are unaffected: they still settle and pay.

## A payout can be delayed

A winning payout waits for the settlement price and for someone to settle. A network outage or a quiet price feed delays the first. A stalled keeper delays the second, although anyone can settle. A delay does not change what you are owed. If no price is published within 14 days of expiry, both sides are refunded instead, and the fee is not returned.

If your wallet cannot receive USDG, the transfer fails and you must claim to another address.

## Contract upgrades and governance

The contracts are upgradeable. An upgrade can change anything, including how funds are held, so a faulty upgrade or a compromised upgrade owner can cause loss of funds. An upgrade takes effect without a delay.

Governance can change the fee within bounds fixed in the code. A guardian can pause new positions. A pause never stops settlement, claims, or refunds.

## Smart contract and network risk

Isometric is a set of smart contracts on Arbitrum and Robinhood Chain. Contracts can have bugs, oracles can fail, networks can halt, and USDG carries its own issuer and technical risk, including the issuer's ability to freeze an address. The protocol is designed to refuse an action rather than perform it on bad data, but no software is risk free.

## This is not advice

Nothing in the app or these docs is investment advice. Isometric does not review whether a trade suits you. Trading leveraged products may be restricted in your jurisdiction, and you are responsible for the taxes on any gains. See the [terms of service](https://app.isometric.fi/terms).

## What the contract code protects

- **Your maximum loss is fixed.** Nothing can be taken from you after you pay.
- **Your payout is funded on day one.** The writer's full collateral for your **Max payout** is locked in the contract when the trade opens, and held until settlement.
- **Nobody picks the price.** Exactly one Chainlink price is valid for a settlement hour, and no participant, including Isometric, can choose another.
- **Claims never expire.** A payout waits for you indefinitely, and you can send it to any address.
- **The trade you sign is the trade you get.** A quote is signed by the writer, and the contract refuses any fill that differs from it.
