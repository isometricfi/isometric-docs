# Open your first position

This walkthrough takes you from an empty wallet to an open position.

<Tip>

To try it without real money, turn on **Demo** in the app header.

</Tip>

<Steps>

<Step title="Connect a wallet" icon="lucide:wallet">

Open [app.isometric.fi](https://app.isometric.fi) and choose **Connect**.

</Step>

<Step title="Join with an invite" icon="lucide:ticket">

Trading is invite-only during the closed beta. Open your invite link, or enter your invite code in the **Join with an invite** dialog.

</Step>

<Step title="Fund it on Arbitrum" icon="lucide:coins">

Isometric runs on Arbitrum One. You need two things in your wallet there:

- **USDG, USDC, or USDT** to pay for positions. Every trade settles and pays out in USDG, so USDC and USDT are swapped to it as the trade opens.
- **A little ETH** for network fees.

Holding money on another network? Open your account menu and choose **Swap (cross-chain)** to bring it to Arbitrum. See [Pay and swap](./pay-and-swap.mdx).

</Step>

<Step title="Pick a market" icon="lucide:search">

The market picker sits at the top of the trade page. Bitcoin, Ether, Solana, and Arbitrum are live.

</Step>

<Step title="Set up the trade" icon="lucide:sliders-horizontal">

The ticket has four controls:

- **Long or Short.** Long pays if the price settles above your target. Short pays if it settles below.
- **Term.** 1, 2, or 3 days, or **Custom** for any whole-hour term from 24 to 72 hours.
- **Amount.** What to spend, counted in USDG. Press the token beside it to pay with USDC or USDT instead. The ticket shows the amount as **Max loss**: the whole cost, fee included, and your maximum loss.
- **Leverage.** 5x to 1000x. Amount times leverage cannot pass a position size of 5,000 USDG.

The quote below the controls updates as you change them. [Direction, amount, term, and leverage](./three-inputs.mdx) explains what each one does to the trade.

</Step>

<Step title="Read the quote" icon="lucide:receipt">

Before you confirm, check the numbers on the ticket. The three that matter most:

- **Worthless at or below** (or above, for a short). Settle on the wrong side of this price and the position pays nothing.
- **Profit above** (or below). Settle past this price and you come out ahead.
- **Max payout.** The most the position can pay, however far the price runs.

The quote follows the live price on its own. [Reading the quote](./reading-the-quote.mdx) walks through every number with an example.

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm" icon="lucide:check">

Press **Confirm Long BTC** or **Confirm Short BTC**. Your wallet asks you to confirm. Paying with USDC or USDT can take more steps. See [Pay and swap](./pay-and-swap.mdx).

The position is open as soon as the transaction lands. You will see it in **Portfolio**.

</Step>

<Step title="Wait for settlement" icon="lucide:hourglass">

Nothing else is required from you. The position cannot be closed early and cannot be liquidated. At the settlement time shown on the ticket, one price decides the result.

If the position pays out, USDG is sent to your wallet when it settles. If it expires worthless, there is nothing to do. See [Payouts and claims](./payouts-and-claims.mdx).

</Step>

</Steps>

## Before you trade

- Read [Know the risks](./risks.mdx). It is short.
- Understand that a position runs to settlement. There is no early exit.
- Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose in full.
