# Open your first trade

This walkthrough takes you from an empty wallet to an open position.

<Tip>

To try it without real money, turn on **Demo** in the app header.

</Tip>

<Steps>

<Step title="Connect a wallet" icon="lucide:wallet">

Open [app.isometric.fi](https://app.isometric.fi), go to **Leverage**, and choose **Connect**. No invite is needed.

</Step>

<Step title="Fund it on Arbitrum" icon="lucide:coins">

Isometric runs on Arbitrum One. You need two things in your wallet there:

- **USDG, USDC, or USDT** to pay for trades. Every trade pays out in USDG, so USDC and USDT are swapped to it as the trade opens.
- **A little ETH** for network fees.

Holding money on another network? Open your account menu and choose **Swap (cross-chain)**. See [Pay and swap](./pay-and-swap.mdx).

</Step>

<Step title="Pick a market and a side" icon="lucide:search">

Press the market at the top left to switch it. Press **Long** if you think the price will be higher at your end time, **Short** if lower.

</Step>

<Step title="Drag the end line" icon="lucide:move-horizontal">

The chart shows the last two days of price on the left and the future on the right. Drag the line marked **Ends** to choose when your trade ends, anywhere from 12 hours to 7 days. You can also press **12h**, **1d**, **3d**, or **1w** under the chart.

</Step>

<Step title="Set your amount and leverage" icon="lucide:sliders-horizontal">

- **You put in.** What to spend, counted in USDG. It is everything you pay, fee included, and the most you can lose. Press the token beside it to pay with USDC or USDT instead.
- **Leverage.** Press it and pick 5x to 100x. Higher leverage pays more per move and loses sooner. **Max** shows the largest amount your leverage allows.

</Step>

<Step title="Read the chart" icon="lucide:chart-line">

The bands on the right of the end line show what each end price pays: **Lose** your amount, **Some back**, profit, and **Max** profit. Hover or tap any price to see exactly what it pays. [Reading the chart](./reading-the-chart.mdx) walks through an example.

</Step>

<Step title="Open it" icon="lucide:check">

Press **Long BTC** or **Short BTC** and confirm in your wallet. The position is open as soon as the transaction lands and shows under the chart in **Open**.

</Step>

<Step title="Wait for the end time" icon="lucide:hourglass">

Nothing else is required. The position cannot be closed early and cannot be liquidated. At the end time one Chainlink price decides it, and any payout is sent to your wallet in USDG. See [Payouts and claims](./payouts-and-claims.mdx).

</Step>

</Steps>

## Before you trade

- Read [Know the risks](./risks.mdx). It is short.
- A position runs to its end time. There is no early exit.
- Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose in full.
