# Payouts and claims

Every winning position, long or short, is paid in **USDG** from money the pool set aside when you opened it.

## Winning position

After settlement your payout is yours to take. It reaches you one of two ways:

- **Automatically.** Isometric's keeper sends payouts to winners' wallets after settlement, in batches. Yours normally arrives within minutes. In Portfolio history it shows as **Claimed**, and the position's details say it was paid automatically.
- **By claiming.** You do not have to wait. Open **Portfolio**, go to the **Claims** tab, and press **Claim**. If you have several claimable positions, **Claim all** pays them in one transaction.

Either way you receive the same amount. A payout never expires. Take it whenever you like.

## Sending a payout to another address

The claim dialog lets you choose where the money goes: **My wallet** or **Another address**. Use another address when you want the payout somewhere else, or when your own wallet cannot receive USDG.

<Warning>
  The whole payout goes to the address you enter and cannot be sent back. Check every character, and
  make sure the address can receive USDG on the network you trade on.
</Warning>

Before it sends anything, the dialog reads your balances and the settlement price directly from the chain through your wallet, and claims exactly that amount.

## When the automatic payment fails

If the automatic payment cannot reach your wallet, for example because the wallet is blocked from receiving USDG, the position shows **Push failed**. Nothing is lost. The payout stays set aside for you, and you can claim it to the same wallet later or to another address.

The keeper retries automatic payments for about a week after settlement. After that, an unpaid payout waits for you to claim it.

## Losing position

A position that settles on the wrong side of its worthless price pays nothing. It shows as **Expired** in Portfolio, and there is nothing to claim or close. What you paid was the whole cost.

## Position statuses

| Status      | Meaning                                                                            |
| ----------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Open        | The settlement time is in the future                                               |
| Settling    | The settlement time has passed and the price is waiting to be recorded             |
| Claimable   | You won and the payout is waiting. It is usually paid to your wallet automatically |
| Push failed | The automatic payment failed. Claim it to this wallet or to another address        |
| Expired     | Settled outside its range, so it pays nothing                                      |

History shows what happened to each position: **Opened**, **Claimed**, **Claimed to 0x…** when you sent it elsewhere, and **Push failed**.

## What Portfolio shows

The account panel summarises your positions:

| Figure                | Meaning                                                                          |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Open value, estimated | What your open positions would pay if they settled at the current price. A guide |
| Open max payout       | The most your open positions could pay                                           |
| Claimable             | Payouts waiting for you                                                          |
| Realized PnL          | Payouts received minus the cost of positions that have paid or settled at zero   |
| Win rate              | Of those positions, the share that paid more than they cost                      |
| Premium paid          | Everything you have paid for positions                                           |
| Paid out              | Everything that has been paid out to you                                         |

Each row links to its transaction, so every step can be checked on the chain.

## Where the money comes from

The pool sets aside the money for your payout when you open the position, so your payout does not depend on the pool having money later.
