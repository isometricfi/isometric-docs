# Payouts and claims

Every payout, long or short, is paid in **USDG** from money the writer put up when you opened it, even if you started the trade with USDC or USDT. To move a payout to another token or network, use the [swap](./pay-and-swap.mdx).

## When a position pays out

Settlement pays you directly. Your payout goes to your wallet in the same transaction that settles the position, normally a minute or two after expiry. The position then shows **Paid**. There is nothing to press.

## When you need to claim

If the transfer to your wallet fails, for example because the wallet is blocked from receiving USDG, the position shows **Won** instead. Nothing is lost. The payout waits in the contract until you take it, and it never expires.

Open **Portfolio**, choose the position, and press **Claim**. The claim dialog lets you choose where the money goes: **My wallet** or **Another address**.

<Warning>
  The whole payout goes to the address you enter and cannot be sent back. Check every character, and
  make sure the address can receive USDG on Arbitrum One.
</Warning>

## When a position expires worthless

A position that settles on the wrong side of its worthless price pays nothing. It shows as **Lost** in Portfolio, and there is nothing to claim or close. What you paid was the whole cost.

## Position statuses

| Status   | Meaning                                                                                    |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Open     | The settlement time is in the future                                                       |
| Settling | The settlement time has passed and the price is waiting to be recorded                     |
| Paid     | Settled and paid to your wallet                                                            |
| Won      | Settled with a payout, but the transfer failed. Claim it                                   |
| Lost     | Settled outside its range, so it pays nothing                                              |
| Refunded | No settlement price within 14 days of expiry, so what you paid, less the fee, was returned |

## What Portfolio shows

| Figure                    | Meaning                                                               |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Open value if expired now | What your open positions would pay at the current price. A guide only |
| Open max payout           | The most your open positions could pay                                |
| Claimable                 | Payouts waiting for you to claim                                      |
| Win rate                  | The share of settled positions that paid more than they cost          |
| Paid in, fees included    | Everything you have paid for positions                                |
| Paid out                  | Everything that has been paid to you                                  |
| Realized PnL              | Paid out minus paid in, for positions that have settled               |

Each row links to its transaction, so every step can be checked on the chain.
