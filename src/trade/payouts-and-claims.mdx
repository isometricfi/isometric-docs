# Payouts and claims

Every payout, long or short, is paid in **USDG**, even if you paid with USDC or USDT. To move it to another token or network, use the [swap](./pay-and-swap.mdx).

## When a position pays out

You are paid automatically. The payout goes to your wallet in the same transaction that records the end price, normally a minute or two after the end time. The position then shows **Paid**.

## When you need to claim

If the transfer to your wallet fails, for example because the wallet is blocked from receiving USDG, the position shows **Won** instead. Nothing is lost. The payout waits in the contract and never expires.

Open **Portfolio**, choose the position, and press **Claim**. Send it to **My wallet** or **Another address**.

<Warning>
  The whole payout goes to the address you enter and cannot be sent back. Check every character, and
  make sure the address can receive USDG on Arbitrum One.
</Warning>

## Position statuses

| Status   | Meaning                                                                                   |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Open     | The end time is in the future                                                             |
| Ending   | The end time has passed and the end price is waiting to be recorded                       |
| Paid     | Ended and paid to your wallet                                                             |
| Won      | Ended with a payout, but the transfer failed. Claim it                                    |
| Lost     | Ended outside its range, so it pays nothing                                               |
| Refunded | No end price within 14 days of the end time, so what you paid, less the fee, was returned |

## What Portfolio shows

| Figure                  | Meaning                                                               |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Open value if ended now | What your open positions would pay at the current price. A guide only |
| Open max payout         | The most your open positions could pay                                |
| Claimable               | Payouts waiting for you to claim                                      |
| Win rate                | The share of ended positions that paid more than they cost            |
| Paid in, fees included  | Everything you have paid for positions                                |
| Paid out                | Everything that has been paid to you                                  |
| Realized PnL            | Paid out minus paid in, for positions that have ended                 |

Each row links to its transaction, so every step can be checked on chain.
