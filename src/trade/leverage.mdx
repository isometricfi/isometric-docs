# What leverage means here

On a perp, leverage is how much you borrow, and it comes with a price at which you get wiped out. On Isometric there is no borrowing and no liquidation. Leverage does two things: it sets how big your position is, and where the **Lose** line sits on the chart.

Pick it from **Leverage** on the ticket: 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 30x, 40x, 50x, 75x, or 100x.

## Leverage sets position size

Your position size is what you put in times the leverage, capped at **5,000 USDG** per trade. Higher leverage lowers the most you can put in, which the ticket shows as **Max**.

| Leverage | Most you can put in |
| -------- | ------------------- |
| 5x       | $1,000              |
| 10x      | $500                |
| 25x      | $200                |
| 50x      | $100                |
| 100x     | $50                 |

The minimum is $1.

## Leverage moves the lose line

Paying less for the same position means more risk. Higher leverage moves the worthless price closer to today's price, and from about 50x past it, so less has to go wrong before you lose it all.

Five three-day Bitcoin longs with Bitcoin at $100,000, each with a $5,000 position size:

| Leverage | You put in | Worthless at | Breakeven | Max payout |
| -------- | ---------- | ------------ | --------- | ---------- |
| 5x       | $1,000     | $83,738      | $100,486  | $1,568     |
| 10x      | $500       | $91,402      | $100,542  | $1,017     |
| 25x      | $200       | $97,388      | $101,283  | $648       |
| 50x      | $100       | $100,466     | $102,476  | $474       |
| 100x     | $50        | $102,936     | $103,965  | $343       |

These are example figures. The chart shows yours.

- **Low leverage** is a safe, small stake in the price. It rarely loses everything and rarely pays many times over.
- **High leverage** pays a lot for a small move your way and loses everything on a small move against you.

**Each 1% past the worthless price pays leverage percent of your amount**, up to the max. At 25x, each 1% is 25% of what you put in. When the market is calm the lines sit closer to today's price. When it is volatile they sit further away, because writers price in a bigger move.

## No liquidation price

Nothing happens to your position while it is open. If a 25x long goes 10% against you on day one and recovers on day two, it pays in full on day three. A perp at the same leverage would have been closed out on day one.

## Choosing a leverage

Ask where you think the price will be at the end time, not how much you want to make.

- If you think it will drift your way or hold, pick low leverage.
- If you think it will move decisively, pick high leverage.

Change the leverage and watch the bands on the chart move before you decide.
