# Leverage with no early liquidation

<Note>
  Isometric is in closed beta on Arbitrum One. Trading needs an invite, and a position is capped at
  5,000 USDG. The contracts are unaudited, so only trade what you are willing to lose.
</Note>

Isometric lets you go **long or short with leverage** on Arbitrum. Every trade is an onchain option: you pay once, up front, and that payment is the most you can ever lose. A wick cannot close you out, because only the price at expiry counts.

## The whole trade in four choices

1. **Direction.** Long if you think the price will be higher at expiry. Short if lower.
2. **Amount.** How much to spend, paid in USDG, USDC, or USDT.
3. **Term.** One to three days. The exact settlement time is shown before you confirm.
4. **Leverage.** 5x to 1000x. It sets your position size, and with it how much every 1% of the move is worth.

At the settlement time a single price decides the outcome. Finish past your breakeven and you come out ahead, up to your **Max payout**. Finish on the wrong side of your worthless price and the position pays nothing.

## Why not a perp

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="No liquidation" icon="lucide:shield-check">
    A move against you does not close your position. It stays open to settlement, however far the
    price wanders in between.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No funding" icon="lucide:clock">
    Holding costs nothing for the whole term. What you pay at open is the entire cost.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payout locked at open" icon="lucide:lock">
    The other side puts up your full **Max payout** when the trade opens. It is held until expiry.
  </Card>
</Columns>

The trade-off is that a position runs to its settlement time. You cannot close it early, and the payout stops growing at the **Max payout** shown on the ticket. [Perps versus Isometric](./trade/perps-vs-isometric.mdx) lays out both sides, and [Prediction markets versus Isometric](./trade/prediction-markets-vs-isometric.mdx) compares it with a yes-or-no price bet.
