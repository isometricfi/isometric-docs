# Prediction markets on your terms

<Note>
  Isometric is in beta on Arbitrum One. The contracts are unaudited, so only use what you are
  willing to lose.
</Note>

On a listed prediction market you pick from questions someone else wrote. On Isometric you write your own: the market, the price, and the end time. Writers quote it on the spot, and the payout is fixed when you place it.

## A prediction in three choices

1. **Your price.** Pick a target above today's price to bet it ends higher, or below to bet it ends lower. Further from today's price pays more.
2. **Your end time.** Any hour from one to seven days out.
3. **Your amount.** What to spend, paid in USDG, USDC, or USDT.

The ticket shows your **Payout** before you place it. If the last Chainlink price at your end time is at or past your target, you get it in full. If it ends short, you lose what you paid and never more.
