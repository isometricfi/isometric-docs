# Trade on your terms

<Note>
  Isometric is in beta on Arbitrum One, and a position is capped at 5,000 USDG. The contracts are
  unaudited, so only use what you are willing to lose.
</Note>

Isometric lets you go **long or short with up to 100x leverage** and no liquidation. You pay once, up front, and that is the most you can lose. A wick cannot close you out, because only the price at your end time counts.

## A trade in three moves

1. **Pick Long or Short.**
2. **Drag the end line** on the chart to choose when the trade ends, from 12 hours to 7 days.
3. **Set what you put in and your leverage**, then press **Long BTC** or **Short BTC**.

Before you trade, the chart shows what every end price pays: where you **Lose** your amount, where you get **Some back**, where you profit, and your **Max** profit. See [Reading the chart](./trade/reading-the-chart.mdx).

## Why not a perp

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="No liquidation" icon="lucide:shield-check">
    A move against you does not close your position. It stays open to the end time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No funding" icon="lucide:clock">
    Holding costs nothing. What you pay at open is the entire cost.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payout locked at open" icon="lucide:lock">
    Your payment and the writer's deposit cover your full max payout from the moment the trade
    opens.
  </Card>
</Columns>

The trade-off: a position runs to its end time, and the payout stops growing at its max. [Perps versus Isometric](./trade/perps-vs-isometric.mdx) lays out both sides.
