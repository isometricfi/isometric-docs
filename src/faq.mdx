# Frequently asked questions

## Trading

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Can I close a position early?">

No. A position runs to its settlement time. That is the trade-off for no liquidation and no funding. Choose a term you are happy to wait out.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I be liquidated?">

No. Nothing happens to a position between open and settlement, however far the price moves against it. What you paid is the most you can lose, and it was paid at open.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there funding?">

No. There is no cost to holding a position.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What decides whether I win?">

One price: the last Chainlink price published at or before the settlement hour shown on your ticket. Above your worthless price pays a long. Below it pays a short.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the most I can win?">

The **Max payout** on the ticket. Every quote has one, fixed when the position opens. Longer terms and higher leverage have a larger maximum.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Am I charged exactly the amount I entered?">

Yes. The amount you enter is the whole cost, fee included, and the ticket shows it as **Max loss**. The trade opens on exactly the quote you confirmed, or it fails and you pay only the network fee.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How many transactions is a trade?">

One. Your wallet first signs an approval for the amount, which is a signature and costs nothing, then sends the trade.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I get paid?">

In USDG, long or short. Settlement pays your wallet directly, normally a minute or two after expiry. See [Payouts and claims](./trade/payouts-and-claims.mdx).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What does Won mean?">

You won, but the transfer to your wallet failed. Nothing is lost. Open the position in Portfolio and claim it, to the same wallet or to another address that can receive USDG. Claims never expire.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why does the confirm button say something other than Confirm?">

It is telling you why the trade cannot go through: the wrong network, not enough USDG, an amount below the minimum, no quote for the trade, or a paused market.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why does the settlement time land later than my term?">

Settlement is your term counted from when you request the quote, rounded up to the next whole hour. The exact time is always on the ticket before you confirm.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What do I need in my wallet?">

USDG to pay for positions and a little ETH for network fees, both on the network you trade on: Arbitrum or Robinhood Chain.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Earning

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="How do I earn?">

By taking the other side of trades. Deposit USDG into the [shared vault](./earn/vault.mdx) and its quoting bot writes trades for you, or quote directly as a [writer](./earn/writers.mdx).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where does the return come from?">

From what traders pay for their positions. There is no lending, staking, or farming.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can the vault lose money?">

Yes. It pays the traders who win, and a strong move in one direction can pay out more than the vault collected.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there one vault for longs and one for shorts?">

No. One shared vault backs both directions.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## The protocol

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Is this an options protocol?">

Yes, underneath. A long is a call spread and a short is a put spread, both fully collateralised at open and settled in USDG on one oracle price. You never need to know that to trade.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who is the counterparty?">

The writer whose quote filled your trade. Its collateral for your full **Max payout** is locked in the contract when the trade opens, so your payout never depends on the writer later.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can Isometric touch my funds?">

Not through day-to-day controls. Governance can change the fee within ceilings fixed in the code. A guardian can pause new positions. Neither can alter an open position, change a recorded settlement price, or stop settlement, claims, and refunds.

The contracts are upgradeable, and an upgrade can change anything. [Know the risks](./trade/risks.mdx) says more.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if the keeper stops?">

Settlement is delayed until someone else sends it. Anyone can. The settlement price is fixed by the Chainlink feed regardless of who records it, so a late settlement pays exactly what an on-time one would.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if the network or the price feed goes down?">

Settlement waits for the price. If none is published within 14 days of expiry, both sides are refunded, less the fee. Nothing already owed to you changes.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
