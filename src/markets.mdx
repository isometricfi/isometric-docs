## What is on offer

| Setting  | Options                                                            |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Target   | Any price writers quote, above or below today's price              |
| End time | Any hour, one to seven days out                                    |
| Amount   | From $1, paying at most 1,500 USDG and at most 10 times the amount |
| Pay with | USDG, USDC, or USDT. See [Pay and swap](./trade/pay-and-swap.mdx)  |
| Payouts  | USDG                                                               |
| Network  | Arbitrum One                                                       |

## Who takes the other side

Every prediction goes out for quotes. Writers answer with a payout, and the largest one fills. The writer locks that payout in the contract when your prediction opens.

## Prices

The chart and the header ticker read the exchange price, which is Coinbase for crypto. Predictions are never priced from the chart. Each quote starts from the **Chainlink** price on chain at the moment you ask, and the prediction settles from the same feed at its end time.
