| Network  | Arbitrum One. The testnet runs on Arbitrum Sepolia |

## Who takes the other side

Every trade goes out for quotes. Writers answer with a price, and the best one fills. Each writer puts up the full **Max payout** for the trades it fills, so every market draws on the same writers and the same [shared vault](./earn/vault.mdx).

## Prices

The chart and the header ticker read the exchange price, which is Coinbase for crypto. Trades are never priced from the chart. Each quote starts from the **Chainlink** price on chain at the moment you ask, and the position settles from the same feed at expiry.
