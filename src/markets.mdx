## What is on offer

| Setting  | Leverage                                                          | Predict                                                    |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Choose   | Long or short, 5x to 100x                                         | Any target writers quote, above or below today's price     |
| End time | Whole hours from 12 hours to 7 days                               | Whole hours from 12 hours to 7 days                        |
| Amount   | From $1, up to a position size of 5,000 USDG                      | From $1, paying at most 1,000 USDG and 10 times the amount |
| Pay with | USDG, USDC, or USDT. See [Pay and swap](./trade/pay-and-swap.mdx) | USDG, USDC, or USDT                                        |
| Payouts  | USDG                                                              | USDG                                                       |
| Network  | Arbitrum One                                                      | Arbitrum One                                               |

## Who takes the other side

Every trade and prediction goes out for quotes, and the best one fills. Your premium and the winning writer's deposit cover the full payout when it opens.

## Prices

The chart and the header ticker read the exchange price, which is Coinbase for crypto. Trades and predictions are never priced from the chart. Each quote starts from the **Chainlink** price on chain at the moment you ask, and each one is decided by the same feed at its end time.
